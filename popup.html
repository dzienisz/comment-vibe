⚠️ Chrome AI not available

Your Chrome version or hardware may not support Gemini Nano:

  1. Use Chrome 138+ on desktop (Windows 10/11, macOS 13+, Linux)
  2. Hardware minimums: 16 GB RAM, >4 GB VRAM, 22 GB free disk
  3. Check chrome://on-device-internals for model status
  4. On Chrome 127–137 only: enable "Prompt API for Gemini Nano" in chrome://flags, then update Optimization Guide On Device Model in chrome://components
🦊 Enable Firefox on-device AI

Comment Vibe can classify tone with Firefox's local AI runtime (Firefox 134+). A small model (~65 MB) is downloaded once; everything stays on your device.

On non-Nightly builds you may also need to set browser.ml.enable and extensions.ml.enabled to true in about:config.
