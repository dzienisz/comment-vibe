Checking on-device AI…
Comment Vibe uses Chrome's built-in AI (Gemini Nano) to analyse the tone of your comments in real time — completely on-device, no data sent anywhere.
⚠️ Chrome AI not available

To enable it:

  1. Open chrome://flags
  2. Search for "Prompt API"
  3. Set it to Enabled
  4. Relaunch Chrome
  5. Visit chrome://components and update Optimization Guide On Device Model
🦊 Enable Firefox on-device AI

Comment Vibe can classify tone with Firefox's local AI runtime (Firefox 134+). A small model (~65 MB) is downloaded once; everything stays on your device.

On non-Nightly builds you may also need to set browser.ml.enable and extensions.ml.enabled to true in about:config.
